Getting around

Pan
drag the background, or middle-drag
Zoom
scroll wheel · shift+scroll to pan
Move a thing
drag a card, or a group by its title bar — it stays where you put it
Fold / unfold
double-click any group
Regroup
drag a node onto a group to put it inside; drop it on empty canvas to pull it out
Inspect
click a node — rename, recolour, set an icon, add notes, draw links
/
search · ⏎ jumps to the first match
F
fit everything on screen
Del
hide the selected node (Hidden brings it back)
Esc
deselect / cancel / leave drawing mode
Draw
opens Excalidraw over the board — shapes stay pinned to what they are drawn on

Everything you change saves itself. Re-running the scanner refreshes what was discovered and leaves your layout, names, colours and notes alone.
